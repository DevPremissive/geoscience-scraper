## The findings that should change what someone builds next

Each is in audit P with the measurement.

**Ontario AFRI PDFs are the pre-2000 scanned era only.** Stratified 60-id sample:
10/12 pre-1990, 11/12 1990s, 2/12 2000s, **0/12 for 2010+**. Modern MLAS-era ids
return `BlobNotFound` and nothing derivable reaches them — not the MLAS
transaction id, the work-report number, a prefix listing, or the metadata index,
which carries no filename. Getting them needs a devtools capture of the
GeologyOntario SPA's own download call, the 1–4 day exercise
`SCRAPERS_BLOCKED.md` scopes for other jurisdictions. **This is the single
highest-value follow-up in the C5 area**: the missing decade is the most
decision-relevant work on any property.

Every retrieval gap is named by id and year, in `coverage_summary()` and in
dossier section 7, because the alternative is section 7 answering "work stopped
in 1998" about ground drilled in 2021.

**The OCR blocker does not exist here.** PLAN_C5 5.1 builds around it; 2% of 303
pages fall below the text threshold because Ontario already OCR'd its AFRI
holdings. No OCR engine is installed and none was needed. Detection stays for
jurisdictions that differ.

**Table extraction fails by every planned route.** pdfplumber and camelot are
not installed and would not help: PyMuPDF's table finder returns **zero** tables
on these ruled-line-free scans. The LLM reads the text of table-dense pages
instead. One consequence is on disk — an extracted intercept reading
`Au 50.000 / Zn 741.000 / As 741.000`, Zn and As identical, misread off a grid,
with `quote_verified: True` because the quoted *header* really is on the page.
**Quote verification proves the text was there, not that the number was read
correctly.** That is why C5.2's gate is human.

**Barren is much harder to extract than mineralized, and the plan treats them as
symmetric.** Over 72 holes: mineralized 16, barren 2, ambiguous 54.
Mineralization is stated in prose because it is why the report exists;
barrenness is a table of blanks or nothing. Two Tier-2 candidates from 72 holes
is not a labelling pipeline. Either barren confirmation needs parsed tables, or
it needs to reason over table *structure* — "no assay row for this interval" —
rather than over prose. **C5.2's stated purpose currently yields less than its
corollary.**

**A reasoning model reported "not found" six times out of six.** `qwen3.6-35b`
spent all 2,000 completion tokens on `reasoning_content` over a 21k-char
context, returned `finish_reason: length` and empty `content`, which fell
through to the not-found default with no error anywhere. Empty content on a
length stop now raises, and thinking is disabled — 8 s versus 113 s for an
equally good answer.

**Dense retrieval cannot find a figure.** Three phrasings of "best assay
results" all missed "assays up to 5.5% Cu over 10m", because that sentence sits
in a chunk about regional geology. A grade is a number against a unit, so the
figure-quoting questions run a lexical pass too — affordable only because the
corpus is per-target (581 chunks). A concrete argument for C5.1's design over
C5.3's, beyond politeness to the publisher.

**The hole → report link was already published.** All 172,259 rows of
`ON_OMEIS_DRILLHOLE` carry a `TECH_ID`. C5.2 step 1 is a join, province-wide.
