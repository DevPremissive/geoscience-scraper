## 3. The pickup measurement

- 129,291 current Ontario claims stand on ground that was previously terminated
- 16,174 are one company re-staking its own ground (excluded)
- 2,911 are partner/JV changes (excluded)
- **110,206 are true pickups** — ground a *different* party let go
- 739 distinct parties have done it; the median one has taken 17 claims

Matched on claim geometry, not on the r7 hex. Matching on r7 gave 1.6 million
'pickups' from 533,210 expiries, because an r7 hex is 5.2 km² and two claims inside
one are not the same ground.
