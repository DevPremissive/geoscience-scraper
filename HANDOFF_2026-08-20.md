## What C2.2 turned out to be

Not a benchmark pull. **For Ontario the CMMI release is the geophysics
modality.** Federal geophysics is blocked behind a portal that needs a browser
(C3.2, unbuilt) and `ON_GEOPHYS` is 246 MB of proprietary Geosoft `.GRD` no
common driver reads. The CMMI grids are ordinary GeoTIFFs covering the province.

**15 rasters now grid onto the Ontario r7 fabric at 96–100% cell coverage, adding
60 features across all 164,577 cells** — gravity and its HGM, the 30 km upward
continuation, magnetics, RTP, RTP-HGM, RTP-VD, deep-source separations, depth to
LAB, depth to Moho, satellite-gravity shape index. That closes a Master §6b
modality gap without waiting for C3.2.

    python src/cmmi.py --status | --verify | --unpack | --ingest
    python src/cmmi.py --gridify ON | --validate-gridify | --benchmark orogenic_au

1.25 GB harvested, all 24 files verified against ScienceBase's published MD5s.

### The fabric is externally validated

The release ships geology aggregated onto **H3 resolution 7 — our fabric's
resolution**, so the comparison is cell-for-cell. Of our 164,577 Ontario cells,
164,220 are in theirs. They carry 20,702 we do not, and **20,280 of those (98%)
sit inside the major lakes `fabric.py` deliberately clips**. Two defensible
answers, not a disagreement. **Cell-set agreement excluding water: 99.53%.**

Coarse lithology agreement is 55.9%, and `validate_gridify` records it as
**inconclusive** rather than as a pass or a failure — 46% of the disagreement is
a single confusion cell (ours metamorphic / theirs intrusive), which is a
taxonomy boundary rather than an aggregation error, and agreement is 66% where
the reference reports no lithological contact against 24% where it reports one,
which is what two *correct* implementations would do.

### The benchmark, honestly

The published surfaces are Zn-Pb; our model is orogenic Au. Against the MVT
surface our model scores Spearman **−0.50**, top-decile overlap 0.024 against
0.10 by chance. The strong negative is the geologically correct answer — Archean
greenstone belts and Phanerozoic carbonate platforms are disjoint ground in
Ontario — and it is evidence the model learned terrane, not a measure of its
accuracy. `cmmi.py` labels every cross-type pairing `cross-system reference`
with a stated prior expectation so the number cannot be quoted as a score.

**A real C2 benchmark needs a published orogenic-Au surface, which this release
does not contain.**

## Bugs found and fixed (all pre-existing, all silent)

Full reasoning in audit N; the short version:

1. **`gridify.write_features` said "append" and overwrote** (N7). C2.2 was the
   first second-producer, and its write left the newest snapshot holding 60
   geophysics features and none of the 239 geology ones. Nothing errored; the
   dossier and viewer would simply have reported bedrock as absent. Now merges,
   plus `carry_forward=` recording `carried_features` vs `computed_features`.
   **Anything that writes features must pass `carry_forward`.**
2. **The raster registry recorded a CRS the file did not carry** (N3/N4).
   `rio_copy(crs=)` is ignored and `open(dst,"r+").crs=` does not take on a COG;
   both failed silently. Fixed with a `WarpedVRT`, a read-back assertion, and a
   skip-path that re-checks the file instead of trusting the record.
3. **`coverage.py` could not see rasters** (N-adjacent). CGMC has read as
   `RAW ONLY` since COVERAGE.md was first generated. Now `RASTER`, with a COGs
   column: 16 COGs across 7 codes.
4. **A bare `except` in my own new code** reported "0 registered COGs" as fact
   when the real problem was a missing import. Removed.

## Gate G1 — a decision, not a build

*"5–10 human-reviewed dossiers for real open ground adjacent to real active
stories, each with a named probable buyer and a defensible price range."*

Named buyers exist (C6.2). **Price ranges do not, and cannot**: they need C6.1
comps, which needs deal terms extracted from material change reports, which needs
the filing PDFs from the mining-scraper A1/A2 campaign. The index gives type and
date only (audit L1).

Two honest options, and this is vis's call:

- **Run G1 without prices.** Judge deal *selection* on its own — is the ground
  right, is the buyer right, is the adjacency real? That is most of the thesis,
  and it is answerable today. The gate's own wording says the fix for a failure
  is "in deal selection (C1/C6), not in more modelling", which is exactly what
  this tests.
- **Hold G1 until comps land.** Cleaner against the gate as written, but blocked
  on a PDF campaign in another project.

The first is recommended. A dossier that names ground, a buyer and a reason to
act is reviewable; a missing price is a stated gap, not a broken dossier.
